How it works

From shelf to suggestion in three steps

1

Lend and record

Librarians lend books, take returns and note each member's 1–5 star rating.

2

Compare every book

LIBRA turns each book's title, authors and top tags into TF-IDF numbers and measures how alike they are.

3

Suggest the next read

Books like the ones a member rated highly rise to the top, minus anything they've already read.
